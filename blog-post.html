How to Prepare for Your First Umrah

  1. Home
  2. Blog Article

Thoughtful preparation can make your journey calmer, more organised and easier to focus on spiritually.

Start with your travel documents

Keep your passport, booking confirmation, flight details and any required travel documents together. Make digital backup copies and verify airline baggage rules before departure.

Plan accommodation and transfers

Check hotel distance, room arrangements, check-in times and the transfer plan between airports, Makkah and Madinah. Families and older travellers may prefer shorter walking distances.

Pack for comfort

Use lightweight luggage and prioritise comfortable footwear, simple clothing, personal toiletries, chargers and clearly labelled medicines you are permitted to travel with.

Leave room in the schedule

A flexible itinerary gives you more space for worship, rest and unexpected delays. Avoid overloading each day with unnecessary travel.
