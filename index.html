How to Verify Our ATOL Protection Status

Taqwa Tours lists ATOL number 74904 . You can verify ATOL protection on the official CAA ATOL website and should request your ATOL certificate after booking where the booking is protected.

Always confirm which parts of your booking are covered, including flights, accommodation and transfers, because ATOL protection may not apply to every travel service.

Package information

What Is Not Included in the Umrah Packages?

Ziyarat tours are generally not included unless specifically stated and may carry an extra charge. Meals also depend on the selected package; some premium packages may include meals while many budget packages do not.

Personal expenses such as local SIM cards, shopping, room service, laundry, additional baggage, Zamzam packaging, insurance and other individual costs are normally separate.

The core package usually focuses on flights, hotels, visa support and transport , subject to the exact booking terms.
